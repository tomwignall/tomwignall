## Engineering philosophy

> **Good platforms hide complexity without hiding capability.**

I believe platform engineering should enable teams rather than constrain
them.

The best platforms provide sensible defaults, automation, security
guardrails and reusable capabilities while allowing engineers to focus
on delivering outcomes rather than repeatedly solving infrastructure
problems.

Technology leadership should work the same way.

My approach is to combine **strategy, architecture and hands-on
engineering understanding** to make technology simpler, safer and easier
to consume.

## The Invisible Engineer

I'm building **The Invisible Engineer** --- an independent engineering

Topics include:

-   Engineering deep dives
-   Architecture
-   Cloud & infrastructure
-   Homelabs
-   Engineering leadership
-   Technology incidents
-   Lessons from when technology goes wrong
